# Repository operating rules

1. All public data must remain synthetic and explicitly marked as such.
2. Do not copy proprietary course prompts, data, screenshots, answers, or grading materials.
3. Do not describe simulation participation as employment.
4. Every metric change must preserve its declared grain, numerator, denominator, unit, and decision use.
5. Every new one-to-many join requires row-count, distinct-entity, unmatched-record, and measure-reconciliation tests.
6. Every experiment change requires allocation, composition, primary-metric, guardrail, and interpretation tests.
7. Do not replace conflicting case results with a single composite score.
8. Generated example outputs must be rebuilt deterministically and verified before release.
9. A green test suite does not justify a real-company or causal claim beyond the stated design.
10. Update documentation, tests, and public-boundary checks with every material feature.
